# Credits

All visuals (skyline, rain, lamp, paper, grain, coffee rings, stamps, corkboard, share cards) are generated in code with CSS, SVG and canvas. The rain sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API. No image, sound or icon assets are used.

The city of Ashport and every sign in it are original.
